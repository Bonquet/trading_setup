# XAUUSD Market Profile

Initial notes (update as evidence accumulates):

- **Trend quality:** aggressive; moves in clean waves during London + NY.
- **Retracement respect:** strong at 50% and 61.8% on H4 after impulsive legs; 38.2% works only in strong trends.
- **EMA behavior:** 50 EMA High/Low on H4 acts as dynamic S/R; clean reclaims often run 2R+.
- **Volatility:** ATR(14) H4 typically 15–30 points during active sessions; Asia often <10.
- **False breakouts:** common during Asia, around news releases, and late NY.
- **Best TFs:** D1 bias, H4 setup, H1 trigger.
- **Best strategy:** Pullback model fits better in most conditions; 50 EMA Williams is excellent on H4 reclaim days.
- **Sessions:** London open (07:00 UTC) and NY open (13:30 UTC) are prime; avoid Asia except for Asia-range context.
- **News avoidance:** skip 30 min before and 30 min after US CPI, FOMC, NFP, retail sales.
